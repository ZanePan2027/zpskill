# Your first task with zpskill: get something usable

[简体中文](first-task.md) · [Install](install.en.md) · [Manual](README.en.md)

zpskill is Zane's Restart Your Life in 24 Hours Skills, helping your AI become a forever buddy by your side. You can start with one current concern, without organizing your whole life or setting up a complete workspace.

After following the [installation guide](install.en.md), start a new chat:

```text
Use zp-life. I am considering changing jobs.
My income is steady, but last-minute requests fill my days.
I want to keep an income and make room for family and my own writing.
Help me identify what I need to clarify, then draft an outline for my next conversation.
Ask for missing facts; do not invent my experience or financial details.
```

This is a fictional illustration, not a user testimonial. Bring your own question about health, rest, a project, or a difficult message. You can also ask the Agent to listen before suggesting anything.

In this example, a usable output might be a conversation outline: what you want changed, what you can offer, what to ask, and how to respond if the request is declined. If missing facts could change the advice, the Agent should ask. You do not need a complete life plan in the first turn.

If the result is generic, ask it to use the conditions you have already given and produce an outline you can actually take into the conversation. A finished draft is not an action taken. Sending, publishing, or making a commitment on your behalf needs your explicit authorization.

Then bring back what happened:

```text
Continue this task. They agreed to reduce the interruptions but have not assigned anyone to take over.
Update the recommendation and the conversation outline accordingly.
```

Agreement is feedback; whether the workload changes still needs observation. To continue across conversations, read [Continue later](continue.en.md). For repeatedly feeling unready, see [Check an avoidance loop](avoidance.en.md). Your normal entry is `zp-life`; [specialized Skills](skill-inventory.en.md) handle tasks such as a résumé directly.
