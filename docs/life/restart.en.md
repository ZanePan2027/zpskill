# Restart Your Life in 24 Hours: your first day with an AI companion

[简体中文](restart.md) · [Install](install.en.md) · [Bring one concern](first-task.en.md) · [Continue later](continue.en.md)

Make today a fresh start. Let your buddy get to know you, understand where you are, explore possible futures, and start one thing you want to do. Keep going together as real life brings new experience.

## Tell your story

After installing, tell an Agent that supports Skills:

```text
Use zp-life. Be my AI companion for life's journey and help me live a life I value.
Let's use today for a fresh start: get to know me, then think about what comes next.
I will share my experiences, current situation, and the life I want.
Listen first and ask about the specific things I bring up. Use what I have already shared; do not invent my history.
```

You can set aside about two hours to talk, use voice, or share existing material. Split it across conversations if that suits you. This is a suggested time budget, not a measured completion time. Start with what matters to you rather than a complete autobiography: what gives you energy, what stays with you, your current situation, what you want to keep, and what you want to change.

## Understand the present together

Ask your buddy to reflect what it has understood about the life you want, your circumstances, hopes, and sticking points. Correct anything it gets wrong. Choose one thing you most want to change.

## Explore a few possible routes

Compare a few paths: why each might fit, what it requires, what could happen, and which you would like to try. Your future can start as a draft and change through experience.

## Do one thing together today

Turn your chosen route into something useful now: a message, an application, time set aside for rest, or an attempt you want to make. If a step depends on someone else, prepare what you can and leave a clear place to continue.

```text
Let's start with this route. Work with me on what we can do today.
Then save the background we confirmed, the usable work, and where we stopped so we can continue tomorrow.
```

When you want, give your buddy a name and share how you like to be accompanied with `zp-partner`. For continuity, authorize a folder for your workspace; see [Continue later](continue.en.md).

End the day with an initial understanding of you, a route you want to take, and a first step begun. Next time, bring back what actually happened and keep going together.
