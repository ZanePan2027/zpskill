# Feeling unready: check an avoidance loop with zp-awaken

[简体中文](avoidance.md) · [First task](first-task.en.md) · [Manual](README.en.md)

When you want to try something but keep preparing because you expect to be rejected or judged unqualified, `zp-awaken` helps distinguish real constraints from predictions you have not checked.

Learning, resting, and building tools can be necessary. They do not by themselves show avoidance. Money, skills, time, health, danger, and an explicit refusal all deserve attention.

```text
Use zp-awaken. I want to try offering a service but feel unqualified.
I keep taking courses and have not shown any work to a relevant person.
Help me separate concrete skill gaps from judgments I made in advance.
Ask one useful question at a time. Do not begin by labeling me as self-handicapping.
```

This is a fictional illustration, not a testimonial or evidence of effectiveness. “I have not completed a full case” is a fact; “therefore nobody will want to look at my work” is a prediction. It could be partly true, or there may be other explanations. A course that fills a specific gap and leads to a clear next step can be worth continuing.

If you want to try, choose one bounded action: for example, finish a small piece of work and invite relevant feedback in an appropriate setting. Agree what prediction it tests, the effort, observations, review window, and boundaries. An Agent can prepare a draft; contacting someone still requires explicit authorization.

This borrows from CBT self-help and collaborative checking. It does not interrogate you, require complete confidence, or replace a worry with a promise that you will succeed. Not every conversation needs homework.

| What happens | What to do with it |
|---|---|
| Someone looks and gives specific feedback | Revise “nobody will look”, then address what they said |
| A real skill gap is identified | Improve the skill or change the route; do not force a positive interpretation |
| No reply arrives | Keep several explanations open, including reach and timing |
| The attempt does not happen | Reconsider the step, conditions, and willingness; not trying does not prove the prediction |

One attempt tests a limited prediction, not your worth. Whether it helps depends on your experience and real changes. The Skill does not diagnose or replace professional treatment.
