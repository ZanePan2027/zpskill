# Skill directory

Start with `zp-life`, your AI companion's everyday entry. It gets to know you, helps you choose, and works with you on what matters today. Use a specific entry below when its purpose fits.

[简体中文](skill-inventory.md) · [Walkthrough](guide.en.md)

## [Main entry](../../skills/zp-life/SKILL.md)

`zp-life`

Use when: Understanding a situation, comparing choices, planning, or completing a task.

```text
Use zp-life. Compare both jobs against my budget and weekly schedule.
```

Output: A route grounded in your goals, possible developments, timing and practical setting, useful outputs, and next steps.

Use the same entry for future directions: “Given my goals and recent changes, show plausible paths, what to prepare now, and when to reconsider.” See [foresight and preparation](../../skills/zp-life/references/future-and-options.md).

## [Question clarification](../../skills/zp-question/SKILL.md)

`zp-question`

Use when: Separating several concerns to identify the task at hand.

```text
Use zp-question. I am worried about changing jobs, income, and time. Help me identify the current decision.
```

Output: The current question, needed judgments, and a request you can continue with.

## [Judgment and decisions](../../skills/zp-judge/SKILL.md)

`zp-judge`

Use when: you must decide whether to change or do something, with thin evidence or a conclusion you already lean toward.

```text
Use zp-judge. I want to re-cut all my next videos but I only have one video and 100+ views of data. Should I?
```

Output: first whether the question holds up; an argument against both doing and not doing; a conclusion (change / don't / observe) with flip conditions and one smallest experiment.

## [Re-check](../../skills/zp-check/SKILL.md)

`zp-check`

Use when: an AI says it is done and you want to confirm, a long chat has drifted, or the AI keeps agreeing with you.

```text
Use zp-check. The AI says it published the article; help me confirm it really did.
```

Output: the AI's claims broken out and graded, checked in ways that do not rely on the AI; a restart brief when things drifted.

## [Self-reflection](../../skills/zp-self/SKILL.md)

`zp-self`

Use when: Exploring motives, values, or recurring choices through experience; feeling ordinary, unsure what you are good at, or sure you cannot because of height, family or schooling.

```text
Use zp-self. Use these experiences to explore what matters to me when I choose.
Use zp-self. I feel too ordinary. Here are a few things I have done: …; what do I have that few others do?
```

Output: Grounded, tentative insights, open questions, and practical experiments; when you feel worthless or without meaning, safety first, then concrete evidence that you matter and one small step; with enough material, also what is distinctly yours, fitting routes, and one small step you can start today.

## [Awareness](../../skills/zp-awaken/SKILL.md)

`zp-awaken`

Use when: You are stuck between two roads (stay or quit), the same problem keeps coming back, you know what to do but cannot do it, you study a lot but do not grow, or you feel “this is all there is”.

```text
Use zp-awaken. I have been at a state-owned company for eight years, dislike it, and dare not leave, but fear regret if I do.
Use zp-awaken. Whenever it matters I back off, even though I know what to do.
```

Output: explore events, patterns, structures, and assumptions as needed. Recognize real constraints before separating facts from predictions. Check the evidence together and, if you want to try, choose a bounded action. The prediction may be supported, weakened, or remain uncertain. Rest, learning, and values are not automatically avoidance. See the [avoidance-loop tutorial](avoidance.en.md).

## [Perspective swap](../../skills/zp-perspective/SKILL.md)

`zp-perspective`

Use when: You want to know how the other side sees it: a partner, boss and employee, buyer and seller, client and vendor, parent and child. Standing inside one position, people often cannot see the root of a matter; stepping into the other's position often makes it clear at once.

```text
Use zp-perspective. I asked her to dinner this weekend; she answered a day later with “we'll see”. How is she thinking?
Use zp-perspective. I am a seller and a customer keeps asking about refunds. Do they not want to buy?
```

Output: a few readings from the other position with reasons, the signals you send and how they may be misread, and one way to check. Understanding only: no manipulation, no mind-reading.

## [Psychological support and therapy assistance](../../skills/zp-support/SKILL.md)

`zp-support`

Use when: Seeking emotional support, feeling stopped by “I can’t”, understanding self-criticism or difficult habits, discussing relationship distress, or preparing for professional care.

```text
Use zp-support. Listen to what has been difficult lately.
Explore it with me when I want to, without assigning an exercise every time.
```

Output: Specific companionship, a revisable understanding, a feasible small experiment, or consultation preparation. Philosophy and meaning are discussed when relevant.

## [AI buddy setup](../../skills/zp-partner/SKILL.md)

`zp-partner`

Use when: You want to name your AI, set what it calls you and how it accompanies you, or define how your ongoing AI buddy should work with you. It does not ask for setup in the first conversation; after a use or two it gently asks once.

```text
Use zp-partner. Help me define an AI buddy that can disagree, explain its evidence, and save progress.
```

Output: A four-line starter card (what to call you, its name, how you want to be accompanied, what you never want to hear) saved in your AI buddy page; when needed, a full identity card with responsibilities, reasoning approach, communication, and memory arrangements.

## [Set up, organize, and continue](../../skills/zp-workspace/SKILL.md)

`zp-workspace`

Use when: Setting up a workspace, organizing sources, saving work, or resuming.

```text
Use zp-workspace. Set up a workspace in this folder, organize access and references to existing material, and continue the current task.
```

Output: Connected direction, personal context, circumstances, routes, cases, and usable outputs, with a clear way to maintain and resume them.

## Owned life knowledge

Keep methods, conditions, counterexamples, and feedback used in real situations. When a similar question returns, the Agent compares those lessons with current conditions to help choose a workable route.

## [External guide evidence connector](../../skills/zp-guide/SKILL.md)

`zp-guide`

Use when you request *The High Value Life Guide*, or a concrete everyday question needs options it can supply. It reads complete relevant entries and returns candidate actions, costs, benefits, and sources to `zp-life` for a plan grounded in your circumstances.
