# Start in chat

Send this with your current situation:

```text
Be my long-term life adviser. Start with the life I want and my current stage.
Consider the people, resources, and circumstances involved. Explore the main
possible directions, recommend a workable route, and explain timing and
practical arrangements. Produce a useful draft or comparison now.
Use what I have already shared and update your judgment when my goals or
circumstances change. Here is my situation: …
```

The first time, you can add: “Call me …; I like it when you say things straight (or gently).” It will remember what to call you and how to speak with you.

To continue in another conversation, ask for a summary of your direction, current judgment, actual actions, usable material, and next dependency. Bring it into the next chat.

In an Agent that supports Skills, [install the workbench](install.en.md) to save sources and progress in one project folder.
